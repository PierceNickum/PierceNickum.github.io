Essential Tools for Painting Warhammer 40k

Here is the basic gear I recommend for assembling and painting your miniatures:

  • Flush Cutters (Nippers): For cleanly removing plastic parts from the sprue.
  • Hobby Knife: For scraping away mold lines before priming.
  • Plastic Glue: For bonding plastic Citadel miniatures together.
  • Brushes: A standard basecoat brush, a fine detail brush, and a drybrush.
  • Wet Palette: Keeps your acrylic paints thin and workable for hours.
